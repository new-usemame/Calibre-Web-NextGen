### Added

- **Sort the Read list by when you finished a book.** With the Read filter on, the sort menu now offers "Date read, newest first" and "Date read, oldest first", so a book you finished today appears at the top even if it was added to the library long ago. Books finished before the date was recorded use their last-changed date. On the Read list, "Newest" and "Oldest" are now labelled "Added, newest first" and "Added, oldest first" (#2800).
