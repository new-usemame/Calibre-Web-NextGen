### Fixed

- **Kobo devices no longer report a sync error from Kobo's reading services when "Proxy unknown requests to Kobo Store" is off.** With the proxy off, the device's token is one CWNG issued, and Kobo rejected every forwarded `checkforchanges` call with 401. CWNG now answers that call itself with the "nothing changed" reply that is measured safe for on-device highlights. Reported by @fabdrol, reproduced by @sgreadly.
