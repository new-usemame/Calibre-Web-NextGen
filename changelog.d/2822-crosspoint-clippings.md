### Added

- **CrossPoint's Sync Clippings works with CWNG.** An Xteink or other CrossPoint reader with Sync Clippings on asked for `/kosync/api/v1/clippings/...`, got an error, and stopped the whole Sync Progress. CWNG now speaks that API: each clipping is kept as a highlight on the book, placed where the reader made it (a short phrase that repeats in the book too), shown in the web reader with the reader's name, and given back to the same reader after it deletes and downloads the book again. Like Kobo highlights since v4.1.48, a clipping goes back only to the reader that made it. Keep the `X-CWNG-Device-ID` custom header on the reader so CWNG can tell your readers apart. CWNG checks the HTTP Basic login on these requests, as on every KOSync route, and CrossPoint 2.0.0 sends no Basic login on clipping requests, so it needs a CrossPoint build that does.
