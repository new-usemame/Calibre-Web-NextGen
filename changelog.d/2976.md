### Fixed

- **Kobos on a nondefault port can renew their token with "Proxy unknown requests to Kobo Store" off.** The OAuth URLs (`oauth_host` and the OpenID discovery document) were built from the request's `Host`, so a Kobo that sends no port in `Host` was sent to port 80 for OAuth while its sync URLs used the external port. When port 80 was a reverse proxy it answered 404, and the device gave up with "sync failed" whenever it needed a new token. They now use the external port like the rest (#2975).
