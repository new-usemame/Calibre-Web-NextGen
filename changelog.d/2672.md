### Fixed

- **More than one user can now be created without an email address.** Previously the first account without an email worked and the second failed with "An account already exists for this email or name", because a blank email was stored as an empty value that the unique constraint treated as taken. A blank email is now stored as "no email", and existing installs are corrected automatically at startup. The error for a genuine clash now says whether the email or the username is already taken. Reported by @bcsteeve, fixed by @sgreadly in #2672.
