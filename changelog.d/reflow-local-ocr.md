### Fixed

- **Reflow keeps legible scanned text selectable when a model reading is unavailable, including conversions without a key or after a spending limit.** Local OCR remains visibly unverified: each paragraph links to its original detail, and the complete original page is available beside it. An existing OCR text layer cannot certify those words.
- **Local recognition uses bounded rasters, execution time and output, records word coordinates and engine scores, and keeps its temporary receipts in the conversion's owned scratch.** Weak or garbled paragraphs retain source images independently of healthy text.
- **An unproved footnote association no longer removes otherwise supported body prose.** Only the uncertain marker or note receives source disclosure.
- **The joint text-quality check scales with passage length, including passages shorter than forty words and image descriptions.**
- **Disputed words receive a second upright pixel read before publication.** Corrections keep their word-level source links, inline emphasis and printed word boundaries. Unsettled optical disagreements can retain just the original word image with an enlarged detail, keeping surrounding prose live. Image descriptions need pixel evidence or use a neutral source label.
- **The image includes a hash-pinned, optical-only Latin-script network (12.5 MB, Apache-2.0) alongside English recognition.** Its larger alphabet can read macron vowels. It is built from Tesseract's `tessdata_best` data once during the image build; conversions never download models. Other installations can run `scripts/install-reflow-latin-ocr.sh TESSDATA_DIRECTORY`. Without that data, disputed readings retain their normal uncertainty disclosures.
- **Paragraph indents keep complete source rows available for checking.** Exact unclaimed opening words return to their own physical line before disagreement checks; a clipped continuation cannot overrule a complete divided-word reading.
- **Short list and index rows keep their own labels and source details.** Complete nearby row evidence can repair a clipped location hint; a merged recognition column cannot repeat neighboring rows as inline print. Final publication checks retain original context for attributed source occurrences without live-word coverage.
- **A complete printed word cannot acquire its own cropped suffix as an extra word.** Matching first-reader and independent whole-word evidence applies regardless of the fragment's length; separate printed occurrences keep their own readings.
- **A number omitted by the first reading needs complete independent print evidence before becoming extra live text.** An unproved numeric fragment stays inspectable as original print; an independently confirmed complete number remains selectable.
