### Changed

- **The Browser reader joins Share my place behind the rollout switch, which remains off by default.** When enabled, an unchecked Browser keeps its own place and read status; a checked Browser shares the latest place, including a move back, and resumes automatically at a newer exact shared place. Browser EPUB places can reach a Kobo exactly when the books align and the Kobo has proven its current KEPUB download. The Devices page always includes the account's Browser and shows its sharing control only when the switch is enabled.
