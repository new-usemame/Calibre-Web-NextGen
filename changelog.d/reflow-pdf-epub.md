### Added

- **Reflow turns a PDF into an EPUB using native text and readings of page images.** On a PDF book's page, Reflow now runs the page-reading converter: clean native pages keep their text, while scans and damaged text can be read by the displayed AI model. It does not ask a model merely to restyle extracted HTML. Source words, reading order, notes, images and uncertainty evidence remain subject to the converter's quality checks.
- **Uncertain output keeps the original page within reach.** When a model reading is unavailable, bounded local OCR can keep text selectable with every word visibly unverified and source-linked. Units that cannot be retained safely use explicitly disclosed original-source images. Original pages and enlarged details remain accessible. Local English OCR provides alignment evidence; the model reads the printed script in the page image.
- **You see the reading cost before consenting.** A local preparation identifies the page images and separates expected cost from the ceilings for first readings, a targeted reread and note-marker readings. The target is $0.50 per book, with a hard maximum of $5 or the administrator's lower limit. Your chosen cap and the shared administrator budget are checked before each new request. Changing the source, scope or cap requires consent again.
- **Reflow works without new provider charges.** Without a key or with paid reading switched off, the same converter keeps native text, reuses compatible cached readings, attempts explicitly unverified local OCR, and discloses source images for unsupported units. It does not switch to an older converter or treat local OCR as a new model reading.
- **Try a sample before converting the whole book.** A sample starts at the first body page found near the beginning of the PDF, or at page 1 when the bounded search finds none. It is downloaded as an EPUB without changing the library. Whole-book output is added as an EPUB format, and replacing an existing EPUB requires explicit consent.
- **The result and report explain what was produced.** Native pages, model readings, unverified local OCR pages, whole-page source disclosures, uncertain readings, reused readings and new requests are counted separately. Confirmed spend and unresolved liability remain separate. Financial details in the EPUB and its sidecar follow the report's visibility setting. Older formatting-review jobs retain their historical labels and counts.
- **Limits stop new spending without hiding unread pages.** A cap or unavailable model leaves completed readings intact, attempts explicitly unverified local OCR for remaining scans, and discloses source images for units that fail the quality checks. Billed unusable replies still count against the cap; ambiguous outcomes keep their holds. Proven-unbilled local failures release their reservations, including after interrupted rejection settlement.
- **Native PDF processing has resource limits.** Supervised workers enforce decoded-stream budgets and absolute deadlines, and password-protected PDFs receive a clear error. Cancellation stops owned work. Fingerprinted preparation checkpoints live in job-owned scratch that interrupted-job recovery and retention remove.
- **Reflow checks the EPUB before filing it.** Every XHTML document must parse, internal links must resolve, and duplicate IDs or unsafe markup are refused. Footnote numbers link back to their own references. Image names match their encoded formats. Whole-book size and text-quality gates run before publication.
- **A failed conversion still appears in the app.** Early worker failures retain their reason in the owner-scoped job list, including after reload. Restarted jobs retain unresolved financial evidence. Samples expire after the configured retention period and say when their download is gone.
- **The Docker image includes local text recognition.** Tesseract and its English and page-orientation data support alignment, source evidence and bounded, visibly unverified local readings. Paid page reading uses the administrator's own OpenRouter key and the shared 24-hour allowance; the default shared allowance is zero.
