### Added

- **A star rating you give a book on your Kobo is now saved as your own rating in CWNG.** The Kobo was sending its ratings straight to the Kobo Store, so they never arrived. CWNG now receives them, stores them as that account's personal rating (the Kobo's 1 to 5 stars become the matching half-star score, and clearing the stars clears it) and no longer passes ratings for books in your library on to the Store. Ratings for Store books still go to the Store. This is one way, Kobo to CWNG: a rating set in CWNG is not sent back to the device. Reported by @eagene (#2248, #2637).
