### Added

- **Force Full Sync now warns that a big library takes many syncs to catch up, and the sync log shows how far there is to go.** A Kobo receives one page of up to 100 books per sync, so after a forced full sync, or a switch between Global Library and My Library, a large library needs many syncs, and forcing it again part-way through starts the walk over. The confirmation on the device page and on the classic profile button now says so. The `Kobo Sync summary` line also ends its counters with `candidates_ahead`, the books in your Kobo scope still past the Kobo's position. It includes books the Kobo already holds, and which books are in scope depends on your shelf and library settings, so it bounds the walk and does not promise how many books the Kobo will receive (#3003).
