### Fixed

- **Covers, the reader and book pages open quickly on very large libraries.** Looking up one book joined all of its authors, tags, series, publishers and languages in a single query that SQLite answered by reading those whole tables first, so on a library of about 270,000 books every cover took around 4 seconds and opening the reader could take 20 seconds or more. Each relationship is now loaded with its own indexed query; on a generated 270,000-book library the lookup went from about 2.7 seconds to under 10 milliseconds. Reported by @victor910 in #2699.
