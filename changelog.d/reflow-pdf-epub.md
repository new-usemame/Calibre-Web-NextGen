### Added

- **Reflow converts a scanned PDF into a real EPUB, with an AI model rebuilding the structure and never the words.** A PDF book now offers "Reflow this PDF with AI…" on its book page, beside Convert. The app reads the PDF itself first and only sends a page to a model when its layout cannot be settled deterministically, so most pages cost nothing. Before anything is sent you are shown what is in the PDF, what the whole book will cost at each model, and a cap the conversion stops at; the conversion starts only once you agree to a figure. Every page the model returns is checked word by word against the page as printed, and a page that has gained, lost or altered a word is refused and keeps the text read straight out of the PDF. The finished EPUB carries a report page saying how many pages were sent, how many were accepted, and what was spent.
- **Reflow can convert a short sample first, so an expensive book can be judged before it is bought.** A sample is downloaded as an EPUB and adds nothing to the library; if it reads well, the whole book is one button away.
- **A footnote in a Reflow EPUB stays a footnote.** Every note opens where it is called, in the popup a reader expects, and the number at the head of the note takes you back to the sentence — on the pages the model rewrote as well as the pages it did not.
