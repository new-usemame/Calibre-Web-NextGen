### Fixed

- **A highlight sent to the Hardcover journal is tagged with the reader it came from, and shows its page.** Every journal entry got the tags `#CWA` and `#Kobo`, so a KOReader or web reader highlight also said Kobo. The second tag now names the source as the Annotations page does (`Kobo`, `KOReader`, `Web reader` or `CrossPoint`), and a source with no known reader gets only `#CWA`. A highlight from KOReader or CrossPoint, or any highlight with a KOReader position, went with no position and showed page 0. CWNG now sends its place in the book, and Hardcover shows the page from it. A highlight from the Kobo showed a page near the start of the book, for example page 10 of 1315 for a highlight at 52%. CWNG sent its progress in the chapter, a value from 0 to 1, as a percent of the book. CWNG now converts the chapter progress to a book percent, as it did before the annotation sync targets were split.
