# Shared spending limit for PDF Reflow

AI formatting review requires an administrator to set a positive **shared AI review budget for the last 24 hours** in Basic Configuration. The default is zero, which disables paid review. Source-only conversion remains available. This limit is separate from each conversion's cap: both must permit a request before it can be sent. The displayed remaining allowance is advisory because another conversion can reserve it first.

Confirmed charges remain in the shared limit for 24 hours after settlement. A request whose bill is unknown retains its entire strict reservation until there is trustworthy evidence of its actual charge or a documented unbilled outcome. Waiting 24 hours or restarting the application does not clear an unknown bill. Reducing the configured limit below existing liability prevents further paid requests; it does not erase existing spend or reservations. A conversion stopped by shared admission still produces its complete source-based output and reports that its optional review was incomplete.

The durable accounting store is `financial-admission.sqlite3` directly under `REFLOW_DIR`. Each attempt has one state row, an exact decimal charge or held bound, and its stable identity. Per-job JSONL records remain the conversion's own report. Admission is serialized across processes with an SQLite write transaction; no network call is allowed until both the shared reservation and the job mirror are durable. The active-window query is indexed; settled rows remain compact idempotency records so a stale job mirror cannot resurrect, duplicate, or erase a charge. No additional scheduled cleanup job is required.

On upgrade, drain or restart older paid workers before enabling new review. An unfinished paid job without the shared-admission marker blocks new admission until its terminal or interrupted-job recovery record exists. Existing job ledgers are reconciled under the same lock before new paid admission. Old confirmed spend outside the window does not consume the current allowance, but unresolved attempts remain held regardless of age. Missing timestamps, malformed/truncated financial records, changed append-only history, conflicting settlements, database corruption, or a backward wall-clock step stop paid admission. None of these conditions prevents disabling the setting, editing administrator configuration, or choosing source-only conversion.

For repair, first disable paid review and stop outstanding Reflow workers so evidence cannot change during examination. Preserve the shared SQLite database and any journal together with all job ledgers before attempting repair. Compare malformed or changed records with a trusted backup and provider billing evidence. Restore missing financial evidence, never replace the shared store with an empty database or delete pending entries to regain allowance. Conflicting evidence leaves a persistent `meta.blocked` flag: clear that flag only after the conflicting job prefix and matching shared attempt states have been independently reconciled from preserved evidence. A database that cannot be validated should be restored from a trusted backup together with the later job records; unknown global reservations must be carried forward. Keep paid review disabled when there is no trustworthy resolution. Direct database repair is an administrator maintenance operation, not part of normal conversion or automatic retention.

Application retention preserves paid, pending, malformed, and conflict job evidence and never sweeps the shared accounting database. Shared status shown to editors contains only availability and the remaining allowance; it does not expose another person's books, jobs, or charge records. The existing feature release gate must also pass before this setting can enable paid review.

Shared-store integrity is witnessed independently in `financial-admission.witness.json`, with a stable identity and increasing generation. `financial-admission.lock` serializes first initialization and each witness/database transition across processes. A new installation durably records `initializing` before it creates the database, commits the matching genesis identity, then records `ready`. Interrupted initialization is unavailable; it is never automatically retried as an empty store. An existing database without its witness also requires maintenance rather than automatic adoption.

Before acknowledging any later SQLite commit, the service durably writes the next witness generation. The database commits that same generation. A missing database, different identity, or an older valid snapshot therefore fails closed, including a lost global reservation made just before its per-job mirror. A crash after the witness write but before database commit intentionally makes paid review unavailable; free conversion and budget administration remain usable. Preserve the witness, lock path, database/journal and job evidence together for repair. Do not delete the witness or reset its generation to make a mismatched database pass. Restore/reconcile all later unknown liabilities from trusted evidence before maintenance resumes paid review. The mechanism detects loss/replacement of the database relative to its witness; a coordinated rollback of the entire directory and all its independent evidence is outside its guarantee.
