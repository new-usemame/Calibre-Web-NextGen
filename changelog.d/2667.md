### Changed

- **Exact Kobo resume can be enabled for larger illustrated EPUBs.** Books over 16 MiB (compressed) always fell back to the percentage popup, with no hint why. The limit is now adjustable with `CWA_KOBO_RESUME_MAX_ARCHIVE_MIB` (1-256, default unchanged at 16), and a book skipped for size logs one INFO line naming the limit and the setting. Refs #2591.
