### Fixed

- **A long series name under a book in the new UI now wraps to a second line instead of being cut off.** Before, the series line on a book card was limited to one line, so a name that didn't fit ended in an ellipsis where the classic view showed it in full. The name now takes up to two lines before it is shortened, the series number stays visible beside it, and the line always reserves two lines of height so cards in a row stay aligned. The full name is still shown on hover (#2147).
