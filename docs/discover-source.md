# Choose where Discover finds books

Discover can pick from your current library, a shelf, or a smart shelf. The source belongs to your account and is shared by the Discover strip, the full Discover page, Classic random previews, and the OPDS Discover feed.

In the New UI, open Discover or use the source selector in the library's Discover section. Choose a source and select **Save Discover source**. In Classic, open Discover and use the same source selector. The initial source is **Current library**, preserving existing behavior. Each surface keeps its existing rules for unread books and visibility.

A shelf limits picks to its members. A smart shelf limits picks to its rules, evaluating personal rules such as read status for your account, so a rule such as “tag does not contain papers” can keep academic papers out of Discover while leaving them available elsewhere in your library. Private shelves belong to their owner; public shelves are offered to other accounts. Smart shelves hidden in Smart shelves visibility are excluded from the source picker; unhide one there before using it. A source never gives access to books your account cannot otherwise see.

An empty shelf produces no picks. If a chosen shelf is deleted, becomes private to another account, is hidden in Smart shelves visibility, or has unusable rules, Discover shows no picks and explains that the source is unavailable. It retains the choice so you can see what changed and choose a replacement. It does not silently fall back to the library. The selector stays available even when there are no picks.

This setting changes suggestions only. It does not change shelf membership, read status, your default library search, reader collections, or access restrictions. Anonymous browsing keeps the library source. Saved searches can be expressed as smart shelves; a separate arbitrary-search source is not part of this change.
