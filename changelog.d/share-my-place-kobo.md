### Changed

- **Kobo devices can be given their own place and read status.** Per-device place and status controls are behind a rollout switch that is off by default. Nothing about how places or read status sync changes until the switch is turned on.
- **Checked Kobos can open at KOReader's exact place**, including a move back, when they hold the current KEPUB and the books align. Otherwise they retain their existing Location with the shared percentage; opening and closing there does not rewind other devices or undo Finished status. Real Kobo reading, including backward moves, reaches KOReader. These behaviours remain off until the rollout switch is enabled.
- **Exact Kobo places require download proof for the current KEPUB.** A queued restore is not proof that the book was downloaded. Missing or unreadable books use the guarded fallback; a Kobo with no held Location receives no shared bookmark, and its first opening does not rewind other devices. Unresolved Kobo requests retain the native Kobo Location. These behaviours remain off until the rollout switch is enabled.
- **Kobo download proof is recorded after a complete full GET body matching its declared byte count.** HEAD requests, byte ranges, interrupted or short downloads, streams without Content-Length, and delegated file transfers leave exact delivery unproven, while annotation restoration keeps its existing behaviour. Exact delivery remains off until the rollout switch is enabled.
