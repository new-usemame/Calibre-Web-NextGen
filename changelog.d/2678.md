### Added

- **Restore covers that exist on disk but never show.** A new admin page, "Restore missing covers", in both the classic and the new interface, finds books with a `cover.jpg` in their folder that the library marks as having no cover, lists them, and restores the cover for the books you select. A select-all control handles large lists. Nothing changes until you confirm, and covers you removed on purpose stay removed unless you select them. Works with split libraries; not available for Google Drive libraries. Reported in #2678.
